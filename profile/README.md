# Morsof

  Based in Casablanca, Morocco, we work with SMEs and international teams in French, English, and Arabic.

  - CRM automation and integration
  - ERP and Odoo integration
  - WhatsApp business automation
  - RAG and internal AI assistants
  - Custom business systems
  - Production infrastructure and monitoring
